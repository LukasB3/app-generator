Rules for using the app generator

You describe a small web app, software builds it automatically, and the result is published on a public page. By sending a request you agree to the following.

  • Do not put personal data, confidential information or passwords into a request. The text is processed by third parties and shapes a page that anyone can open.
  • Do not request apps that imitate other companies or people, collect data from their users, or contain unlawful, hateful or sexual content.
  • Do not try to overload or circumvent the service's limits.
  • Generated apps come without any warranty. They are demos, may contain mistakes and may be removed at any time without notice.

Generated apps and how to report one

The apps are written by an AI model from visitors' requests and published at lukasb3.github.io/app-generator-apps without prior review by a person. Each one is checked automatically, cannot contact other servers, and carries a badge marking it as AI-generated. They do not reflect my views. Each app stays online for one day, or for ten days if it was requested from an account, and is then removed.

If an app infringes your rights or is unlawful, email me its address at bossert.dev@gmail.com. I remove such apps without delay once I learn of them.

The captcha

To keep automated abuse out, the request form and the login form load Turnstile from Cloudflare, Inc. (USA). Turnstile runs a check in your browser and transmits your IP address and technical browser information to Cloudflare, which returns a result that my server verifies, or, for a login, the provider that keeps the accounts. This is necessary to protect the service and its budget (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

Sending a request

When you send a request, my server (rented from Hetzner Online GmbH, located in Nuremberg, Germany) receives the text and your IP address. It stores the text, the time, the progress of the job and a keyed hash of your IP address, which lets it enforce the limit per visitor without keeping the address itself. If you are logged in, the record also holds the identifier of your account. These records are deleted after 30 days. The server keeps no access log with IP addresses.

The text of your request is sent to Anthropic, PBC (USA) through its API, because its AI model writes the app. Anthropic does not receive your IP address from me.

I process this data to provide the service you asked for and to protect it against abuse (Art. 6 (1) (b) and (f) GDPR).

Published apps

The finished app is stored in a public repository and served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA). Anyone who has the link can open it, and its files are publicly visible. The app is derived from your request, so anything you wrote there may show up in it. When you or someone else opens an app, GitHub processes the visitor's IP address to deliver it.

Apps are removed automatically after one day, or after ten days if you were logged in when you requested them. You can ask me to remove an app you requested earlier than that by emailing me its address.

An account

The app generator works without an account. If you choose to log in, you enter your email address and receive a mail with a link and a code; there is no password. Your address is used to log you in and for nothing else: no newsletter and no notifications.

Accounts are kept for me by Supabase (Supabase Pte. Ltd.) in a database in Frankfurt, Germany. It stores your email address, an identifier for the account and the times of your logins, and it sees your IP address when you log in. The login mail is sent through Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc., USA), which receives your email address for that purpose. On my own server, the requests you send while logged in carry the identifier of your account, so that the page "My apps" can list your apps and keep them online for ten days. For as long as such an app is online, my server also keeps its files, so that you can change the app with another request.

I process this data to provide the account you asked for (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR). The account stays until you ask me to delete it: write to me from its address and I delete it together with the apps that belong to it.
