About this site

This website is a private, non-commercial project. It demonstrates how software can build small web apps from a description.

Rules for using the app generator

You describe a small web app, software builds it automatically, and the result is published on a public page. By sending a request you agree to the following.

  • Do not put personal data, confidential information or passwords into a request. The text is processed by third parties and shapes a page that anyone can open.
  • Do not request apps that imitate other companies or people, collect data from their users, or contain unlawful, hateful or sexual content.
  • Do not enter personal data into a generated app. Some apps store what visitors enter, and everything they store is public.
  • Do not try to overload or circumvent the service's limits.
  • Generated apps come without any warranty. They are demos, may contain mistakes and may be removed at any time without notice.

Generated apps and how to report one

The apps are written by an AI model from visitors' requests and published at lukasb3.github.io/app-generator-apps without prior review by a person. Each one is checked automatically and carries a badge marking it as AI-generated. It cannot contact other servers, with two exceptions: an app that keeps data its visitors share can reach its own tables in a database, and an app that shows the weather or exchange rates can ask my server for them. They do not reflect my views. Each app stays online for one day, or for ten days if it was requested from an account, and is then removed. A few apps that I requested myself are shown on the start page and stay online until I take them off.

If an app infringes your rights or is unlawful, email me at bossert.dev@gmail.com. Please name the app's address, say why you consider it unlawful, and give your name and email address. I confirm that your report has arrived, look at it myself, remove such an app without delay and tell you what I decided.

The same address is the single point of contact under Articles 11 and 12 of the EU Digital Services Act, for authorities and for users. You can write in German or English.
